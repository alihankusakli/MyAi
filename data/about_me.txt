We don’t stop.

We may fail, fall, lose, or face disappointment — but stopping is not an option. We are human. We are not perfect. We feel everything, but we always get back up and continue.

We improve constantly.
We don’t sacrifice long-term goals for short-term pleasure.
We value people, but we reserve true loyalty and unconditional support for a select few.

We confront our weaknesses directly.
If something can be improved, we work on it. If it can’t, we accept it and move forward.
Before others judge us, we’ve already judged ourselves.

We try. Always.
We don’t need to love everything or succeed at everything — but we refuse to stay ignorant by not trying. Difficulty is not a reason to quit.

If we want something, we pursue it. At minimum, we make the attempt.
